Full Description / About the App

Unit Converter V Pro is a modern, lightweight, and lightning-fast Android utility application built using Jetpack Compose. Designed with a clean and intuitive user interface, it provides instant and accurate conversions for your daily calculation needs without requiring an active internet connection.

Whether you are a student, engineer, or everyday user, this app makes converting units effortless across multiple essential categories.

Key Features & Categories Supported

  • Weight & Mass: Seamlessly convert between Kilograms (kg), Pounds (lbs), Grams (g), Ounces (oz), and Stone (st).
  • Length & Distance: Quick conversions for Kilometers (km), Miles (mi), Meters (m), Feet (ft), Inches (in), and Centimeters (cm).
  • Temperature: Accurate conversions between Celsius (°C), Fahrenheit (°F), and Kelvin (K).
  • Volume & Liquid: Convert Liters (L), Milliliters (mL), and Gallons (gal) with ease.
  • Data Storage: Handy conversions for Megabytes (MB), Gigabytes (GB), Kilobytes (KB), and Terabytes (TB).

Privacy & Offline-First Architecture

  • 100% Offline Capability: The app functions entirely locally on your device. No personal data, inputs, or calculation histories are ever uploaded or stored on any external servers.
  • Smart Local Preferences: Utilizes local device storage (SharedPreferences) to remember your last-used categories and preferred units for a smoother user experience.
  • Secure & Safe: Designed with strict adherence to modern Android guidelines and secure, non-intrusive AdMob advertising integration.

Privacy Policy - ViDUEVA
Last updated: August 28, 2026

At ViDUEVA and its operating divisions (including ViDUEVA Labs) ("we," "our," or "us"), we deeply respect your privacy and are committed to complete transparency. This Privacy Policy governs our official main website, digital services, and the practical solutions delivered through our operational units.

1. Information We Handle

Operational Solutions & Development Services: Through our active divisions (such as ViDUEVA Labs), we build custom web pages, software applications, and digital solutions. Any project-related data or user interaction handled through these services is managed with absolute confidentiality.

Core Research & Future Expansion: As ViDUEVA evolves to introduce new technological projects, AI systems, or online platforms, we ensure that any necessary data collection (such as basic user credentials or preferences) remains strictly limited to what is required to operate and improve those specific services.

Offline & Privacy-First Architecture: Many of our current tools and applications are built to operate locally on user devices, ensuring that personal data remains secure and inaccessible to external parties.

2. How We Use Information

Any information processed across our platforms or services is used strictly for:

- Operating, maintaining, and upgrading ViDUEVA Labs' applications, web development solutions, and digital tools.
- Managing software updates, user authentication (where applicable), and project communications.
- Supporting our internal research, development, and strategic technological expansions.

3. Data Confidentiality & Third-Party Sharing

No Selling of Data: We never sell, trade, rent, or lease personal or client data to any third parties, external organizations, or advertisers.

Trusted Infrastructure: To sustain our development and maintain digital platforms securely, we utilize standard monetization frameworks (such as non-intrusive AdMob banner ads on applications) and secure cloud infrastructure operating under strict privacy standards.

4. Data Security

We implement robust technical and organizational security measures to protect all systems, websites, and data associated with the ViDUEVA brand against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

5. Future Scalability & Policy Updates

As ViDUEVA grows and introduces new projects or divisions in the future, this Privacy Policy will be dynamically updated to reflect those technological expansions. Any revisions will be posted directly on this page.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions, project inquiries, or legal concerns regarding this Privacy Policy or the scope of ViDUEVA's operations, please reach out to us through our official main website channels.
